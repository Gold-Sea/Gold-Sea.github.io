abstract: >-
    This work presents SpecRL, extending speculative decoding into a dynamic, online approach
    that allows for real-time adjustments based on the evolving
    state of the rollout process. Our method dynamically adapts
    the speculative decoding strategy in response to the current system load and periodically updates the draft model
    to maintain alignment with the target model’s evolving parameters.
